How I work

Ask one question

Start with a single operational question, such as "how long do devices last?" or "where does the fuel go?"

Go to the source

Pull from the production databases with read-only queries, not from someone's exported spreadsheet.

Use the right model

Handle censoring, lags and bias properly, because a plain average can be misleading.

Ship something visual

Deliver a map or report that anyone can open, anonymized when it needs to be shared.

About me

I'm Ameen Elzaki, a data analyst working in fleet telematics in Saudi Arabia. My day-to-day work is on the operations side of a GPS tracking company: devices, installers, vehicles and the data they produce.

My background is in statistics and quantitative finance: time series, state-space models, econometrics and valuation. I now apply those methods to operational problems such as device reliability, field-service costs, fuel efficiency and trip accuracy.

I care about answers people can actually use, so most of my work ends as a single interactive page that a manager can open on a phone, in Arabic or English.
